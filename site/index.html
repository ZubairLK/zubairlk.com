My approach

I start by understanding your business, your team and the tools you already use. There’s no one-size-fits-all setup.

Then we focus on the biggest, quickest wins. That might mean learning to use Claude well, improving an existing process or building something new.

My goal is to help you grow revenue without adding headcount and reduce admin overhead. I test changes with the people doing the work and stay involved as your needs change.

FAQs

I’m completely new to AI. Can you help?

Yes. You don’t need technical knowledge or a plan. We can start with the basics, from uploading your first file to using AI for something you do every day.

Can you help me and my team get more from ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes. We’ll work on your own tasks, so you and your team learn how to ask better questions, use files and check the answers.

Can you help with the tools I already use?

Yes. We start with the tools you already use and look at where AI could help. That might mean using them differently or connecting them to cut down on manual work.

Do I need custom software?

Not necessarily. Often, existing tools are enough. I only recommend custom software when there’s a clear reason to build it.
